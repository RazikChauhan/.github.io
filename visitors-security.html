THE VISITOR JOURNEY

Three steps. One shared record.

The resident, guard and admin each see the part of the visit relevant to their role.

01

Before arrival

A resident registers an expected guest, delivery or service visit for their unit.

02

At the gate

A guard reviews the visit in the guard workspace and records the check-in.

03

On departure

Check-out closes the visit, leaving an entry and exit history for authorised staff.

ROLE-BASED WORKSPACES

Clear tasks for everyone at the gate.

The visitor workflow connects the unit, the gate and the community office without a separate paper register.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Visitors & Security FAQs

Can residents register deliveries?

Yes. Expected visits can include guests, deliveries and service people.

Who records arrival and departure?

The security guard uses the guard workspace to record check-in and check-out.

Can an admin review previous visits?

Authorised community staff can review the gate history associated with their community.

Where does a resident start?

A resident chooses the relevant property and adds the expected visitor details.
