COMMON QUESTIONS

Community Planner FAQs

Does the planner calculate savings?

No. It uses the selected unit count to plan records and a simple one-bill-per-unit cycle.

What if our billing rules are more complex?

We can review separate charges, exceptions and periods during setup.
