# SOCMGT Unit-Based Pricing Model

## Purpose

SOCMGT will use a **simple unit-based annual pricing model**.

All paid customers receive the **complete standard SOCMGT platform**.

The subscription price increases only according to the number of units managed by the community.

The pricing model should be presented clearly on the marketing website as:

> **Everything Included. You Only Pay for Your Community Size.**

There should be no confusing feature-based packages where one customer gets Billing but another does not get Staff Management, Visitor Management, Committee Management, etc.

The core rule is:

> **Same complete SOCMGT platform. Different annual price based on community size.**

---

---

# Important Pricing Rule

All paid tiers receive the same complete standard SOCMGT software functionality.

Do not create feature restrictions such as:

- Essential gets Billing but not Staff Management
- Professional gets Committee Management but Essential does not
- Enterprise gets Visitor Management but Professional does not

This is NOT the SOCMGT pricing model.

The SOCMGT model is:

> **More units = higher annual price.**

The functionality remains the same.

# Important Commercial Disclosures

The following items should not be presented as included inside the annual subscription unless explicitly approved:

- Payment gateway/provider fees
- SMS usage beyond included allowance
- WhatsApp Business/provider charges
- Physical access-control hardware
- Boom barriers
- RFID readers
- RFID tags
- Biometric devices
- Security guard phones/tablets
- CCTV hardware
- Smart meters
- Custom third-party integrations
- Custom development
- Large custom migration projects
- Dedicated enterprise infrastructure
- Custom enterprise SLA

Use a simple website note such as:

> **Prices are annual and GST is applicable. Payment gateway/provider charges, SMS/WhatsApp usage, optional hardware, custom integrations and custom development may be charged separately.**
