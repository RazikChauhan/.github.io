## Brand & Style

The visual personality combines the clinical precision of Swiss rationalist typography with the stability of institutional enterprise software. It addresses residential community boards, real estate asset managers, and property directors who demand financial integrity, administrative compliance, and absolute reliability. 

The aesthetic leverages high-density data legibility, restrained layouts, and quiet visual authority. Instead of hyper-saturated tech gradients or informal consumer patterns, the system prioritizes clarity, structural hierarchy, and crisp surface delineations. The emotional response is one of operational command, security, and administrative calm.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high-contrast legibility and clear semantic function:

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal blue acting as the primary action driver, focal point anchor, and brand accent. Used for interactive triggers, active navigation states, primary buttons, and key workflow vectors.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Midnight slate anchoring typography, primary headers, structural framing, and deep institutional container zones.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`)**: Strategic emerald/teal representing fiscal accuracy, operational uptime, verified visitor status, and positive delta values across ledger metrics.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Balanced slate scale supporting secondary copy, structural rules, container borders (`#E2E8F0`), and tinted architectural backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`).

Canvas contrast remains strictly light-mode first, relying on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces grounded against cool off-white page grounds to establish optical planes without visual noise.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on the systematic versatility of Inter. Tracking is intentionally tightened on large displays (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) to remove glyph drift and reproduce the deliberate authority of printed corporate editorial layouts.

- **Scale & Ratio**: Built on a modular 1.250 (Major Third) scale, tuned for dense architectural layouts and corporate data tables.
- **Weights**: Restricted to Regular (400) for high-cadence reading body text, Medium (500) for structural metadata, Semi-Bold (600) for labels, interactive elements, and subheadings, and Bold (700) exclusively for top-tier value propositions and display titles.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Uppercase micro-labels leverage increased letter spacing (`0.04em`) to maintain legibility when indicating status, technical modules, or platform categories.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a standard 12-column fixed grid anchored to a maximum content width of 1280px (`80rem`). Spacing steps follow an absolute 8px baseline rhythm to maintain strict mathematical harmony across marketing containers and in-app interactive mockups.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) page margins. Workflow strips and comparative matrices take full 12-column spans or balanced 6/6 and 4/8 splits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px page margins. Complex grids (such as 4-column feature grids) collapse to 2x2 configurations.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margins. Stacks vertically; workflow comparison tables switch to tabbed or swipable card representations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses low-contrast physical outlines paired with ambient, multi-layered diffuse shadows. Rather than harsh directional drop shadows, the system mimics natural, diffused office light reflecting off calibrated matte surfaces.

- **Border Strategy**: All interactive surfaces, cards, and modal elements feature a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`. This anchors the perimeter cleanly against pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or off-white (`#F8FAFC`) backdrops.
- **Low Elevation (Resting Cards, Role Cards)**: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Mid Elevation (Hover States, Mega-Menus, Flyouts)**: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **High Elevation (Mockup Frames, Book-a-Demo Lead Card)**: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The system implements a soft, disciplined corner profile (`roundedness: 1`). Elements do not use full pill geometry except for specific status badges, reinforcing an orderly, architectural foundation.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges, Table Rows)**: 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius. This gives form fields and operational controls a clean, precise finish.
- **Surface Containers (Cards, Comparative Matrix Rows, Lead Forms)**: 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Product Mockup Frames (Desktop Viewports, Tablet Panels)**: 12px (`0.75rem`) corner radius (`rounded-xl`).
