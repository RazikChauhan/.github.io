Commercial Disclosures & Clarifications

To maintain full commercial transparency, the following external third-party services and hardware items are not bundled within the base annual software subscription and may be charged separately based on actual usage:

Payment Gateway Fees Online payment provider MDR/charges are settled directly through your chosen banking gateway.
SMS & WhatsApp SMS or WhatsApp Business API usage beyond the standard platform allowance is billed at carrier cost.
Gate Hardware Physical hardware such as boom barriers, RFID readers, RFID tags, and biometric devices are separate.
Guard Tablets & CCTV Security guard phones/tablets, smart meters, and CCTV camera hardware remain community assets.
Custom Integrations Bespoke third-party software integrations and custom engineering requests are scoped on demand.
Dedicated SLA Custom multi-township enterprise SLAs or dedicated private cloud infrastructure are available on proposal.

Frequently Asked Questions About SOCMGT Pricing

Do smaller societies get fewer features than larger societies?

No. All paid SOCMGT customers receive the exact same complete standard platform. Whether you have 40 units or 400 units, you get the full suite of billing, security guard app, visitor tracking, complaints, resident apps, and committee governance without feature locks.

Is there an onboarding or software setup fee?

No setup fee. Every paid plan comes with free initial onboarding and assisted Excel data migration to ensure your resident directory, flat numbers, and opening balances are imported seamlessly.

How does the annual subscription billing work?

Subscriptions are billed annually in advance according to your total contracted community unit size (e.g., ₹15,000/year for up to 50 units, ₹30,000/year for up to 100 units). Applicable GST is charged as per government regulations.

Does SocMgt hold our maintenance collections?

No. SocMgt never holds or escrows your funds. Payments made by residents via online gateways settle directly into your housing society's verified bank account.

What happens if our society expands or adds more units?

You can easily upgrade to the next unit tier at any point during your annual cycle. We calculate the difference on a prorated basis so your society only pays for the active period.

Are payment gateway charges included in the subscription?

Payment gateway provider fees (MDR) and transactional SMS/WhatsApp beyond the included allowances are separate from the annual software subscription and depend on your chosen payment aggregator.
