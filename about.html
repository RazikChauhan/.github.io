apartment OUR STORY & MISSION

Empowering Residential Communities Across India

SocMgt was built to bring clarity, trust, and accountability to housing society operations, committee governance, and resident life.

WHY WE STARTED

Built for the unique complexities of Indian housing societies

Managing an Indian apartment complex or gated community involves juggling disparate WhatsApp groups, paper receipt books, vendor payments, gate logs, and strict state co-operative society bylaws.

Committee members volunteer their personal time and face immense administrative overhead. SocMgt was engineered to unite property records, maintenance billing, complaint tracking, and visitor security into one transparent, intuitive platform.

What guides our software design

Total Financial Transparency

Every maintenance rupee is accounted for. Clear dues calculation, traceable digital receipts, and real-time ledger visibility for treasurers and residents.

Resident Privacy First

We believe residential data belongs strictly to the society. No intrusive third-party ads, no selling member data, and strict role-based access controls.
