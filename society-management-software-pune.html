MAHARASHTRA CHS RULES

Designed for Pune Co-operative Housing Societies & Fed Bye-laws

Housing societies in Pune operate under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies (MCS) Act 1960. From Model Bye-law 67 reserve fund mandates to annual audit preparation and Form I & J member registers, generic tools fall short.

Mandatory Sinking & Repair Fund Ledgers

Configured strictly according to Maharashtra bye-laws (0.25% sinking fund and minimum 0.75% repair fund per annum of construction costs).

Non-Occupancy Charges Compliance

Enforce the state-mandated cap of maximum 10% on service charges for rented flats, eliminating owner-tenant calculation disputes.

Water Tanker & Seasonal Utility Billing

Built-in tracking for private water tanker expenses and variable PMC water cess distribution across towers in Baner, Kharadi, and Wakad.
