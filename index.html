COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions from community teams

A quick guide to who SocMgt is for and how the workflows fit together.

Who can use SocMgt?

Community administrators, committee members, owners, tenants, residents, security guards and staff have role-specific views. Access is assigned by the community.

Can one person manage multiple properties?

The linked-properties workflow is designed to show each unit under one account, with dues and requests in the context of the selected property.

How does maintenance billing work?

The community chooses billing frequency, due dates and charge rules. Admins create bills against units; members can review their dues and payment history.

How do residents join their community?

Community admins can invite members and review registration requests before access is granted. Ask us about QR-assisted registration for your community.

Can my community connect its own payment account?

SocMgt is designed around community-owned collections. We can walk through the payment account options and setup for your society.

Can different roles use the same platform?

Yes. Community admins, residents, committee members, guards and staff each see the information and actions relevant to their role.
