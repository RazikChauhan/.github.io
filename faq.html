COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions FAQs

Who can use SocMgt?

Community admins, owners, tenants, residents, committee members, guards and staff use role-specific views.

Can one person be linked to several units?

Yes. Linked properties are designed for a person with more than one property under one account.

Can our society bill owners instead of tenants?

The community can configure the payer type under its maintenance billing settings.

How do residents raise complaints?

A resident submits a complaint or maintenance request, which an admin can assign and track.

How are visitors handled?

Residents can register expected visits and guards can record arrival and departure.

Can we import existing records?

Community setup includes planning for units, people and relevant financial opening information.

What about each society’s payment account?

Discuss community-owned payment account setup and supported options during onboarding.
